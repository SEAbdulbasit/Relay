## Behavior and limits

Multi-agent tasks retain bounded factual tool receipts across a same-process Stop or result-save failure. A successful storage-only repair and predecessor drain can make those receipts available to the next planner, navigator, executor, critic or synthesis call. Repair accepts no action closure and cannot repeat a write. Receipt identities include the journal run, attempt, accepted reply and byte-exact original provider ID; logical-task identity is a separate context fence. A later accepted reply may legitimately reuse a provider ID.

Receipts are data, never executable tool calls, source permission or independently verified goal completion. Pending receipts stay out of inference. Whole entries are bounded to 32 retained receipts and a 128 KiB summary/evidence/artifact-text budget with a 512-byte allowance per entry; this is not a measurement of all resident object memory. At most six receipts/8,000 bytes enter each context, with eight source proofs and explicit omission/coverage text. Insufficient room even for coverage stops inference. New tasks clear the prior task's cache. Restored history cannot reconstruct this live cache or original app authority.

Source-free calculations/app-launch reports and positively proved selected regular files have continuation adapters. Selected-file canonicalization, metadata, bounded content reads and hashing run off MainActor. Before private model admission and on both successful and failed replies, source/configuration/task fences are checked again. Changed bytes, symlink targets, grants, configuration or task identity cannot admit an old private receipt. Unsupported app/browser/native/knowledge/calendar receipts and their derived facts are omitted until those adapters are qualified.

One physical file-proof worker retains its slot until its actual IO returns. Cancellation or timeout ends its waiter promptly, releases the model lease and discards late proof results; it does not close an in-flight descriptor from another task. Another file proof gets a bounded busy refusal while that physical work settles. Synchronous kernel IO cannot be promised to stop immediately. Production proof deadlines remain unchanged.

Successful explicit storage repair or Continue preflight clears only its own storage error. A later unrelated error, even an identical-looking string assigned by another caller, remains visible. Failure/reset/logical-task identities prevent an old repair from clearing a newer failure or acknowledging a replacement task's receipts. Failed repair preserves the error and performs no inference or effect.
