## 1. Executive Summary

Between upstream project 2.8 (`6214cc8`) and upstream project 3.1 (`9e2f6c6`), upstream landed a major product evolution centered on **Magic Reply** (rebranding and expanding Double Tap), **Physical Notch Tap** (hardware ambient-light + accelerometer + gyroscope + lid-angle tap sensing with interactive calibration and `Tools/LidSensorLab`), **Sidekick Notch V3 ("Lumen")** (`Take Over · Magic Reply · Summarize · Proofread` action bar, 250 ms modifier hold-to-talk, click-to-jump active work window, single 30 Hz lighting clock, Home-focus completion suppression, recovery voice dictation), **Popup-Free In-Place Proofread** (passive AX bookmark + normal Copy/Paste preserving browser/Electron Undo + Home draft snapshot routing), **Plugged-In Idle Analysis Continuation** (`PluggedInAnalysisScheduler`), and **Onboarding / Settings 3.1** (`Notch & Shortcuts` pane, `NotchTouchCalibration`, interactive `OnboardingMagicReplyDemo`).

At the same time, auditing `Sources/Relay/` reveals **six critical architectural blockers and release gates** that currently prevent Relay users from experiencing Reference-grade autonomy and personal intelligence even where underlying primitives exist:

1. **Computer Use is compiled out of Release builds and locked to 2 of 6 backends** (`Sources/Relay/NativeTaskControlDependencies.swift:17-34`, `Sources/Relay/SettingsView.swift:516-522`), requires ChatGPT.app's internal `SkyComputerUseClient` (`Sources/Relay/OwnedNativeComputerUseClient.swift:124-140`), rejects non-ASCII Unicode typing (`OwnedNativeComputerUseClient.swift:59-61`), and fails **33/33 live ordinary-window control runs** due to a 750 ms metadata ceiling (`NativeOrdinaryWindowPolicySource.swift:107`) and strict `children.count` digest comparison (`NativeStandardControlSurvey.swift:176, 208-213`) that trips `policy.changedContext` whenever AppKit/Chromium lazily materializes AX children.
2. **Codex CLI broke on ChatGPT.app updates and Claude CLI is completely missing**: `Sources/Relay/CodexCLIClient.swift:46, 122` enforces a hardcoded 2-element `approvedCodeHashes` set (`20db7eba...`, `a11ce331...`) even though lines 127–141 already verify Apple Developer ID `2DC432GLL2` and identifier `"codex"` via `SecStaticCodeCheckValidity`. Meanwhile, `MOD-05` (`ClaudeCLI`, `claude -p --output-format stream-json --json-schema`) has zero implementation in `Sources/Relay/`, and `LocalInferenceLease` (`Sources/Relay/LocalInferenceRouter.swift:16-36`) immediately throws `LocalInferenceFailure.busy` instead of queueing overlapping chat, quick-action, Magic Reply, or OCR work.
3. **Personal Sources are session-ephemeral or manual backup imports rather than live checkpointed pipelines**: `Sources/Relay/NativePersonalSources.swift:88-106` holds at most 8 AppleEvents-selected Notes/Mail items in session memory; OAuth 2.0 + PKCE remote MCP (`CON-01..03`) and curated cloud connectors (Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, Slack, Notion, Granola) are missing from `Sources/Relay/DirectMCPConnector.swift:34-61`; and `PluggedInAnalysisScheduler` does not exist yet.
4. **Knowledge & Proactive Intelligence stop at per-file quotes and regex TODOs**: `Sources/Relay/GeneratedKnowledgeVault.swift:26, 58-137` caps runs at 8 files of excerpt highlights without a multi-slice semantic `Overview.md` + `People/Projects/Plans/Preferences/` vault synthesis, `writingstyle.md` has a path stub (`SidekickNotchOverlay.swift:65-67`) with no `WritingStyleCollector`, `saveGiftKeepsakeToDesktop` returns `nil` (`SidekickNotchOverlay.swift:94-95`), and `ProactiveSuggestions.swift:135-160` scans lines for literal TODOs instead of Reference's 3-stage **Cross-Source Judge → Read-Only Live Research → Editable Morning Artifact Cards**.
5. **Quick Actions & Double Tap reject browsers and lack 3.1 Notch capabilities**: `Sources/Relay/FocusedDraft.swift:167-193` requires `AXRole == "AXTextArea"`, a writable `AXSelectedText` attribute, and caret at exact `utf16.count` end — rejecting Chrome/Safari/WebKit `contenteditable` boxes (Gmail, Slack web, WhatsApp web) and mid-draft edits. `Sources/Relay/BoundTextSelection.swift:26-35, 55-58` explicitly blacklists all browser bundle IDs (`!webContent`, `!Self.browserBundles.contains(...)`), forcing Proofread into copy-only mode in every browser. `DocumentSummarySource.swift:16, 47-48, 139` caps text at 8 KB / 20 PDF pages and refuses YouTube transcripts and window-image + text synthesis.
6. **Home, Onboarding & Settings hide key workflows or lack lifecycle teardown**: Morning Brief cards are hidden behind a sheet (`MainView.swift:26, 150-152`) instead of surfaced on Home; Home composer lacks inline Proofread/Summarize routing and smooth mic-into-draft (`MainView.swift:149`); `SettingsView.swift:516` hides window control behind `#if DEBUG`; and full app-owned clean Uninstall (`SYS-10`) is unimplemented.
